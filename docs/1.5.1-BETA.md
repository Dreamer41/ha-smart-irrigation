## What's New

### 1. Mulch Adjustment for Routine Watering

A new **Mulch** select entity and paired **Mulch ET Adjustment** slider let you account for water loss from bare soil between plants, without hand-tuning crop coefficients.

**When to use it:**
- Bare soil is exposed between plants
- Young trees or shrubs without a full canopy yet
- You want to adjust for mulch or soil cover without changing Kc

**How it works:**
- Default: "Mulched" (no adjustment, existing zones unchanged on update)
- Set to "Not Mulched" to scale the routine weekly target from -50% to +70% (negative for soil that holds water, such as heavy clay or shade)
- Default adjustment: 20% (reflects typical bare-soil evaporation in warm climates)
- Rain credit remains independent—rainfall is always credited at 100%
- Works with both interval-based and ET curve demand models
- Does **not** affect deep soak (different purpose)
- Survives restarts and updates

**Why this matters:**  
Hargreaves-Samani ET₀ is calculated for a *well-watered reference crop*, which implicitly assumes vegetated ground. Bare soil loses significantly more water to direct evaporation (50–80% more in that layer alone). If you apply bare-reference ET to bare soil without adjustment, you'll underwater. This adjustment is the right place to make up that difference, not in the crop coefficient.

### 2. Home Assistant 2025.1+ Background Task Compatibility

Fixed a race condition in the fertilizing reminder test when Home Assistant 2025.1+ runs time-change listeners as background tasks. The test now correctly waits for background tasks to complete (`wait_background_tasks=True` in `async_block_till_done()`).

**Impact:**
- Full compatibility with Home Assistant 2025.1.4 (latest at release time)
- No changes to running integrations or user-facing behavior
- All 535 tests pass on both HA 2024.3.3 and HA 2025.1.4

## Update Your Dashboard

The **Mulch** select and **Mulch ET Adjustment** slider are new entities that can be added to the ZoneFlow card. Existing dashboards work unchanged; the defaults mean existing zones are not affected.

### To add Mulch controls to your dashboard:

1. Open your ZoneFlow dashboard, or create a new one
2. Edit the zone's ZoneFlow card
3. Click **Show code editor**
4. Add these two lines under the zone's entity config:
   ```yaml
   - entity: select.<zone>_mulch
   - entity: number.<zone>_mulch_et_adjustment_pct
   ```
   (Replace `<zone>` with your zone's entity prefix)
5. Click **Save**, then **Done**

### Or use the AI Setup Guide:
Paste [AI_SETUP.md](../AI_SETUP.md) into Claude or your favorite AI assistant and ask:
> "Update this Home Assistant dashboard YAML for ZoneFlow 1.5.1: add the Mulch select and Mulch ET Adjustment slider to the zone's card."

The guide includes a ready-to-paste card example.

## After the Update

**Nothing extra to do—just restart Home Assistant.** Your existing zones will continue with the default "Mulched" setting, so no watering behavior changes until you explicitly enable the Mulch adjustment.

If you have bare soil or young plants without a canopy, open your zone's device page → Configuration → Mulch, switch to "Not Mulched", and tune the slider based on your observations over the first week or two.
