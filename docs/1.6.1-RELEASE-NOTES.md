# ZoneFlow 1.6.1 — Rain without a gauge, Resume Automatic and a ready-made dashboard

**What's new**

- **Weather Underground rain (experimental)** for outdoor zones **without a
  rain gauge**: ZoneFlow borrows rain from 1-3 private weather stations
  within 2 km of your home (nearer is better). Only for rain deduction, not
  100 % reliable. **Before you start:** first look at the Weather
  Underground map for stations near your garden that report rain; second,
  the free API key needs your own station uploading at least temperature and
  humidity. If you have a rain gauge you don't need this. Guide:
  [WEATHER-UNDERGROUND.md](WEATHER-UNDERGROUND.md).
- **Manual rain**: for zones without a gauge, enter rain you read by hand
  (Manual Rain + Add Manual Rain, or the `zoneflow.add_rain` service, up to
  14 days back).
- **Forecast Skip Hit Rate**: how often a watering skipped for forecast rain
  was followed by real rain, judged 48 hours later.
- **Resume Automatic** (greenhouse and indoor zones): a button that gives a
  device you switched by hand back to ZoneFlow at once. **Auto Resume** and
  **Auto Resume After** (0.5-24 h) replace Manual Hold Time: a forgotten
  device goes back to automatic, or waits for the button if you switch Auto
  Resume off. A heater you switched on by hand is always taken back when the
  inside temperature goes above the vent temperature.
- **A ready-made dashboard**: Settings → Dashboards → Add dashboard → New
  dashboard from scratch → ⋮ → Raw configuration editor, and paste:

  ```yaml
  strategy:
    type: custom:zoneflow
  ```

  You get an overview tab and one tab per zone, built from your zones each
  time it opens.
- **Several crops in one greenhouse**: *Add integration → ZoneFlow → Add a
  crop to a greenhouse*. Each crop has its own valve, probe, plant and
  schedule and uses the greenhouse's climate and inside sensors. If you set
  up separate greenhouse zones per crop before, join them to their
  greenhouse under each zone's *Configure → Greenhouse*. See
  [GREENHOUSE.md](GREENHOUSE.md).
- Adding a second zone now starts with a small menu: *Add a zone* or *Set up
  Weather Underground rain*.

## Update your dashboard

Do a hard refresh of the browser (Ctrl+F5) after updating to load the new
card. The built-in ZoneFlow card shows the new entities by itself:

| Entity | Zones | Card |
|---|---|---|
| `number.<zone>_manual_rain`, `button.<zone>_add_manual_rain` | outdoor, no rain gauge | controls |
| `button.<zone>_resume_automatic` | greenhouse, indoor | controls |
| `switch.<zone>_auto_resume`, `number.<zone>_auto_resume_after` (replaces `number.<zone>_manual_hold_time`) | greenhouse, indoor | settings |
| `sensor.<zone>_forecast_skip_hit_rate` | outdoor with a weather service | diagnostics |

A greenhouse's card now lists its crops by itself. A crop's
`sensor.<zone>_greenhouse_status` and `switch.<zone>_greenhouse_control` are
hidden (a crop has no climate devices): remove them from a hand-built
dashboard if you placed them there.

On a hand-built dashboard, add them to your existing cards the same way as
before (Edit dashboard → the card → Show code editor), replacing
`number.<zone>_manual_hold_time` with `number.<zone>_auto_resume_after`, or
paste [AI_SETUP.md](../AI_SETUP.md) into an AI assistant and ask it to update
the dashboard for 1.6.1. **Or use the new auto-generated dashboard above.**

## After updating

Restart Home Assistant. Your old Manual Hold Time carries over as Auto Resume
After (rounded up to the next half hour, at least 0.5 h). Weather Underground
rain does nothing until you set it up.
