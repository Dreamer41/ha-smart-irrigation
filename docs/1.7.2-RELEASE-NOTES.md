# ZoneFlow 1.7.2 — Watered by the rain, drag and drop plants

**What's changed**

- **Watered by the rain.** Once an hour (and a few minutes after a restart) a zone
  works out what its next routine watering would give, on its own settings. When
  the rain since the last watering is already more than that, the zone counts as
  watered: its interval starts again, and the Status and Why? say so ("Routine
  watering counted as done at 14:00: the rain (19.8 mm) was more than the 14.0 mm
  it would give"). Nothing runs and no litres are counted. Rain beyond the one dose
  carries to the next watering, and the same rain is never counted twice. A paused
  or busy zone is left alone.
- **Deep soak by the rain.** The same check looks at the rain since the last deep
  soak: the zone's deep soak target within 24 hours, or the target plus 5 mm within
  72 hours, counts as the deep soak (and as the routine watering, as a deep soak
  does).
- **Mark Deep Soak Done**, a button for zones with no rain measuring (no rain gauge,
  no Weather Underground): press it when you saw enough rain fall or soaked the
  bed by hand. It is hidden where rain is measured.
- **Drag and drop plants.** On the overview card, **Move plants** shows each zone's
  plants as chips; drag a chip onto another zone (mouse or finger). Every drop asks
  first: where to, what becomes of a main plant already there (keep it, make it a
  record, archive it, or swap), and whether to use this plant's watering settings
  too (then every plant in that zone is watered that way). A zone with no valve
  takes the plant as a record only. The Move button in the Plants popup works as
  before.
- **The Status says how much the next watering is expected to give**: "Next
  watering Mon 05:30, estimated 12 mm".
- **Next Irrigation Estimate is hidden** (it keeps working and can be unhidden under
  the entity's settings): Days Until Next Run says the same.

## Update your dashboard

No dashboard changes needed. The new button and the Move plants toggle show by
themselves; do a hard refresh of the browser (Ctrl+F5) to load the new cards. A
hand-made dashboard that lists Next Irrigation Estimate still shows it.

## After updating

Nothing: just restart Home Assistant. Right after the restart a zone whose rain
already covers its next dose will count as watered by the rain.
