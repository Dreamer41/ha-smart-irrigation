# ZoneFlow 1.7.3 — Days between waterings, Watering time, Delete zone, Valve Overlap

**What's changed**

- **Days between waterings are settings.** Two new sliders next to the weekly
  targets in a zone's settings: **Routine Days Between Waterings** (normal and
  cool weather, 4 to start with) and **Routine Days Between Waterings (Hot)**
  (3 to start with), 1 to 10 days each. They were fixed before. Want a shorter
  gap in hot weather, or a longer one in general? Move the slider. A longer gap
  gives a bigger dose each time, so the weekly target is still met; if one
  watering then no longer fits the zone's *Routine Max Safety Runtime Cap*, Check
  my setup says so. Days Until Next Run and the Status follow a change at once.
  Copied with a zone's settings and presets.
- **Watering time on the zone card.** A new **Watering time** button: when the
  routine watering (and the deep soak, where it is on) starts, at a set clock
  time or before or after sunrise or sunset with an offset in minutes. Save
  changes it at once. It is the same setting as Configure > Settings, one tap
  away. (Saving is refused while the zone is watering.)
- **Delete zone button.** At the bottom of the zone card's Settings popup
  (administrators only): *Delete this zone*, then a confirmation. The same
  removal as Delete on the device page.
- **Valve Overlap, for zones that share a pump.** A new setting (0 to 10 seconds,
  0 = as before) in the Safety group: when a zone finishes and another zone is
  waiting for the same pump, the next zone's valve opens first and the finishing
  zone's closes that many seconds later, so the pump never pushes against shut
  valves and the pipes do not bang. Not used for service runs or flow
  measurements, or when both zones share one flow meter. Shown only where a pump
  is shared.
- **The rain that starts the dry-down is a setting.** Three new sliders in the
  Rain group: **Dry-Down Rain Threshold (24h)**, **(4 days)** and **(7 days)**,
  starting at 35, 50 and 100 mm, the values that were fixed before. Rain over any
  of them starts both dry-down holdoffs. Shown in your units; hidden where no rain
  is measured; copied with a zone's settings and presets.
- **Services.** `zoneflow.set_schedule` and `zoneflow.delete_zone` (for
  automations and scripts). Both are for administrators, and both act only on the
  zone they name: `delete_zone` never picks "the only zone" for you.
- **One place works out the routine dose.** The routine watering, the hourly
  "watered by the rain" check, the Status estimate and Why? now share the same
  calculation, so they can never disagree about how much a routine watering gives.

## Update your dashboard

No dashboard changes needed. The new buttons and sliders show by themselves; do a
hard refresh of the browser (Ctrl+F5) to load the new cards. A hand-made dashboard
that lists settings by hand does not show the new sliders until you add them.

## After updating

Nothing: just restart Home Assistant. Every new setting starts at the value that
used to be fixed (gaps 4 and 3 days, rain thresholds 35 / 50 / 100 mm, Valve
Overlap 0), so nothing changes in how your zones water until you move one.
