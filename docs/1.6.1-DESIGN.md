# ZoneFlow 1.6.1 design proposal

Draft for review, revision 2, 4 Oct 2026. Nothing here is coded yet. Model used for this draft: Opus 5.5.

## 0. Scope

| Item | Size | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A. Manual rain entry | small | 1.6.1 |
| B. "Did the skip pay off?" journal | small | 1.6.1 |
| C. Weather Underground nearby stations (experimental, optional) | large | 1.6.1 |
| D. Auto-generated dashboard (strategy) | medium, frontend only | 1.6.1 |
| E. "Resume automatic" button and auto-resume time | small | 1.6.1 |

Build order: A, B, C (rain), then E, then D (frontend last, so its tabs include every new entity).

## 1. How rain works today (what everything builds on)

- One optional rain gauge entity per outdoor zone (`rain_counter_entity`), a tip counter. `calc.track_tip_total` handles counter resets; tips x `rain_mm_per_tip` = a cumulative mm total.
- That total goes into `RainWindowTracker` (`rain_tracker.py`), a list of `(time, cumulative_mm)` samples. Every rain figure is read from it: `today_rain_mm()`, `rain_windows()` (15 min ... 14 d), `rain_since()`.
- Users: the 30-minute "raining now" check before and between pulses, rain credit in the routine plan (`rain_day_history_mm`), the deep-soak 14-day threshold, the forecast dry-override, summary and sensors.
- Forecast skipping stays as it is: it uses Home Assistant's own weather entity. Nothing in 1.6.1 changes it.

## 2. A. Manual rain entry

For people who read and empty a simple gauge by hand.

- Entities (outdoor zones only): number `Manual Rain` (0-200 mm, step 0.5) and button `Add Manual Rain`. Service `zoneflow.add_rain` with `amount_mm` and optional `when` (default now), for automations and voice.
- The number resets to 0 after the button is pressed.
- Adding rain adds it to the zone's rain record at `when` and logs "Manual Rain Added".
- **Hidden when the zone has another rain source.** If a rain gauge or Weather Underground is set for the zone, the number and button are created hidden (the same way other unused entities are hidden today). The person can unhide them in Home Assistant if they want them.
- If someone unhides them and enters rain on top of another source, the day's credit becomes the higher of the two, not the sum, so nothing is counted twice.
- `when` in the past: the tracker today ignores out-of-order samples, so a small `insert(ts, mm)` is added that shifts later samples up by `mm`. Entries older than 14 days are refused.

## 3. B. Skip journal ("did the skip pay off?")

- Every time a watering is skipped because the weather forecast said rain, store `{skipped_at, kind, forecast_mm}`.
- 48 h later fill in `actual_mm = rain_since(skipped_at)` from whatever rain source the zone has (gauge, manual, WU). Mark it **paid off** if `actual_mm` reached the skip threshold, otherwise **missed**.
- Keep the last 30 entries in the state store.
- Sensor `Forecast Skip Hit Rate` (%, last 30 days) with the entries as attributes. One summary line: "Forecast skips: 7 of 9 paid off this month".
- A zone with no rain source records "no rain data" and the entry is not counted.

It tells the person how much to trust their forecast service for skipping.

## 4. C. Weather Underground nearby stations (experimental, optional)

### 4.1 What it is and what it is not

- An **optional, experimental** way to borrow rain readings from private weather stations near the garden, **only for people without a rain gauge** who want reasonably accurate local rain history.
- **People with a rain gauge do nothing with WU.** Their rain is already handled locally by ZoneFlow. A zone with a gauge is never offered WU, and the docs say so at the top: "Have a rain gauge? You don't need this."
- It is only for **rain deduction** (how much rain to credit against watering). It does not do forecasting and does not replace the weather entity used for skipping.
- Why bother: forecast services often use a station 20 km or more away. A private station within a few km is usually much closer to what fell in the garden. But it is still **not 100 % reliable**, and less so the further the stations are from the zones. The docs say this plainly.

### 4.2 Before you start (docs and AI_SETUP, stated first)

The docs, `AI_SETUP.md` and the first setup screen all start with these two steps, so nobody wastes time:

1. **First, check the Weather Underground map** (wunderground.com) for stations near your garden (within a few km) that report rain. If there are none, stop here: use your own gauge, the manual entry, or nothing.
2. **Second, to get a free API key you must upload data yourself.** Register your own station on wunderground.com and send at least outdoor temperature and humidity (no rain gauge or full weather station needed). The easy way is the [ha-weather-uploader](https://github.com/lancer73/ha-weather-uploader) HACS integration; a plain web-request example goes in the docs too. The API key then appears in your WU account.

Three separate secrets: Station ID and Station Key (to upload, used by the uploader, not ZoneFlow) and the API key (to read rain; the only one ZoneFlow stores).

### 4.3 Setup: one shared WU entry

- The "Add integration" flow starts with a choice: **Add a zone** or **Set up Weather Underground rain**. One WU entry per Home Assistant.
- WU setup: API key -> ZoneFlow asks WU for stations near Home Assistant's home location -> shows the stations within the radius with distance and today's rain -> the person picks **1, 2 or 3 stations** (as many as the area has).
- The key is stored only in that config entry: never in YAML, never logged, redacted in diagnostics.
- The WU entry creates diagnostic sensors: one per station (rain today, last report time, distance) and one combined `WU Rain Total`. The person can compare them with what they see before trusting it.
- Outdoor zones **without a rain gauge** get an option **Use Weather Underground rain** (on/off). Zones with a gauge don't see it. If a gauge is added later, the gauge takes over and WU is switched off for that zone.
- Radius: default 1 km, slider 0.5-2 km (changed in review: nearer is better; stations over 1 km are marked "compare with your own rain"). Stations further out are not offered. If none is in range, setup says so and stops. Distance is worked out by ZoneFlow from the station coordinates.

### 4.4 Polling

Dynamic, driven by the zones' own schedules:

- **Quiet mode: every 2 hours.**
- **Watering mode: every 10 minutes**, from 1 hour before any WU zone's next watering until that watering has finished. The WU poller asks each zone using WU for its next routine and deep-soak time (`next_watering()`, already in the controller) and switches mode by itself.
- **Watering started by hand** (button or service): one immediate poll at the start, then 10-minute polls while it runs.
- **Plus one poll at 23:55 local time** to catch the day's last rain. (The roadmap said "just after midnight", but WU's current-reading total resets at midnight, so at 00:05 the old day's total is already gone. 23:55 avoids needing a second WU API.)
- Calls: about 36 a day in quiet mode, plus about 21 per watering window with 3 stations. Even if zones watered all day, 10-minute polling is about 430 a day, so any number of zones stays inside the free key's daily limit (to confirm against the current WU terms before coding).
- After errors, retry on the next slot. A repair issue appears after 6 hours with no good data and clears itself when data returns.

### 4.5 Turning station readings into one rain figure (as built)

Changed during coding: combining per-poll rises with "the lower of two" lost rain whenever two stations uploaded a few minutes apart. Built instead (wu_logic.py):

- Each usable station's **daily total** (rain since its midnight) is combined: 3 = median, 2 = the lower, 1 = as is.
- What the combined total grew by since the last poll is the rain added.
- When a station drops out or comes back, only the stations present both times count for that poll, then the figure is re-anchored, so no fake jump.
- Left out: no report for 2 h (or still yesterday's total), a jump over 150 mm, or no rise for 24 h while another station rose (stuck).

### 4.6 What WU rain is used for

In quiet mode WU rain arrives in 2-hour lumps. If such a lump went into the short "raining now" windows (15, 30 and 60 minutes), it could stop a watering because of rain that fell up to 2 hours earlier. Dynamic polling solves this around waterings:

- WU rain always **counts for** today's rain credit, the 24 h to 14 d windows, deep soak, the forecast dry-override and the skip journal.
- A WU rise counts for the **15/30/60-minute "raining now" checks only when it is fresh**: the previous good reading from the same stations is no more than 15 minutes older. That is true in watering mode (10-minute polls) and never true for a 2-hour lump.
- The first poll of watering mode (1 hour before) is a 2-hour lump, so it does not count as "raining now". The polls after it do. By the time the watering starts there are 5 fresh polls, so the 30-minute check sees real, recent rain.
- Technically: a WU zone has no gauge, so its rain record holds only WU samples, each marked fresh or not. The long windows use all of them; the short windows use only the fresh ones.
- Limit: a station uploads about every 5 minutes, so "raining now" from WU lags real rain by up to about 15 minutes. Fine for its purpose (rain history and deduction); the docs mention it.

### 4.7 Docs

New `docs/WEATHER-UNDERGROUND.md`, and a WU section in `AI_SETUP.md` and the README:
- the two "before you start" steps from 4.2, first and in bold;
- it is experimental, optional, only for rain deduction, not 100 % reliable, better the closer the stations are;
- how to pick 1-3 stations and what the confidence wording means;
- use the skip journal and the per-station sensors to judge whether it is good enough for your garden.

## 5. E. Resume automatic

### What it does today
In a greenhouse or indoor zone, when someone switches a fan, vent, mister or heater by hand, ZoneFlow leaves that device alone (a manual hold) for the **Manual Hold Time** slider: 0-480 minutes, default 60, where 0 means no hold at all. The hold only ends when that time runs out.

### New
- **Button `Resume Automatic`**: ends every manual hold in the zone at once and re-runs the climate decision straight away. Logged as "Automatic Resumed".
- **Switch `Auto Resume`** (default on, so updating changes nothing):
  - **On**: the hold ends by itself after the time on the slider below, so a forgotten device goes back to automatic.
  - **Off**: a hold lasts until the button is pressed.
- **Slider `Auto Resume After`**: 0.5-24 h, step 0.5 h, default 1 h. Hidden while Auto Resume is off.
- The old minute slider is replaced by these. On update, the stored minutes are converted to hours (rounded up to the nearest 0.5 h, at least 0.5 h), and a zone with 0 ("no hold") gets Auto Resume on with 0.5 h. The "no hold" option is dropped. The old entity is removed by the existing entity cleanup.
- Status line while held: "Fans: manual until 18:30" (Auto Resume on) or "Fans: manual until you press Resume Automatic" (off).

### Safety (unchanged by the hold setting)
- A mister switched on by hand is still switched off after Max Misting Per Hour, whatever the hold setting. Water running unsupervised is never left on.
- A heater switched on by hand is taken back by ZoneFlow if the inside temperature goes above the zone's high setpoint, whatever the hold setting. Logged as "Manual Hold Ended: too hot". Without this, a forgotten heater could overheat the greenhouse.

## 6. D. Auto-generated dashboard

- A dashboard **strategy** in the card's JavaScript (`custom:zoneflow`): Home Assistant builds the dashboard from the current zones each time it opens, so new zones appear by themselves.
- Views: an **Overview** tab (every zone's status line, next watering, today's rain) and **one tab per zone** with the ZoneFlow card, which already shows the right controls for outdoor, greenhouse and indoor zones.
- Creating it: Settings -> Dashboards -> Add dashboard -> new from scratch, then paste a 3-line YAML (`strategy: type: custom:zoneflow`). Newer Home Assistant versions may list custom strategies in the add-dashboard dialog; to be checked on 2024.3 and 2025.1. The person can later "take control" to edit it by hand.
- Tab names and texts use the card's existing language dictionary; new strings go into the Gemini round.
- Testing: unit tests can only check that the strategy produces the right view list; only you can check how it looks.

## 7. Testing plan

- Unit tests per feature: tracker `insert`, hidden manual entities when another source is set, higher-of-two rule, journal fill-in after 48 h, WU combining (median / lower of two / single / none, midnight reset, stuck, silent, spike), quiet/watering polling switch from `next_watering()` and on manual start, only fresh WU rises in the short windows, 23:55 poll, coordinator with a mocked WU API, config-flow WU branch, key redaction in diagnostics.
- Sandbox: manual rain by button and service on a test zone; skip journal by faking a forecast skip. WU against the real API only if the sandbox gets a key; otherwise mocked only.
- New card and entity strings: one Gemini round for all card languages and translation files.

## 8. Decided (from Juha's review)

- Manual rain entities are hidden when a gauge or WU is set; the person can unhide them.
- WU is only for zones without a rain gauge; gauge users never see it.
- Forecast skipping stays on Home Assistant weather; WU is only for rain deduction.
- WU is experimental and optional; docs say it is not 100 % reliable.
- Dynamic polling: every 2 hours normally, every 10 minutes from 1 hour before a WU zone's watering until it ends, plus 23:55.
- 1, 2 or 3 stations; two stations use the lower reading.
- Docs and AI_SETUP state the two "before you start" steps first.
- Dashboard strategy and Resume Automatic both go into 1.6.1.
- Resume Automatic has an Auto Resume on/off and a 0.5-24 h time that replaces Manual Hold Time; "no hold" is dropped.
- A heater held on by hand is released above the high setpoint.
