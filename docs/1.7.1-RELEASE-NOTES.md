# ZoneFlow 1.7.1 — Water now fix, quick setup and nursery plants

**What's changed**

- **Water now waters.** The *Water now* button (Run Routine Irrigation Now) used
  to do nothing, without a word, when the routine was not due yet. It now waters
  at once and counts as the routine watering done. Pause, snooze today, frost,
  rain falling right now and the runtime caps still apply.
- **Quick setup.** Adding an outdoor zone has a **Quick setup** box (ticked). It
  then asks only for the valve, how the water is delivered, and how much water
  the zone gives (number of emitters, the flow of one, and the area they wet).
  There is no default flow: drip and sprinklers differ a lot, and a rough figure
  is fine because **Calibrate the flow rate** on the zone card measures it
  exactly later. The sensors ZoneFlow clearly found are used, and the plant
  type and the default climate give the rest. Everything can be changed later in
  Configure. Untick the box for the full set of questions, as before.
- **Nursery plants: ready to move.** Follow a plant towards the day it can go to
  its bed. In the Plants popup, a seed-raised type (tomatoes, chilis, leafy
  vegetables, herbs, strawberries, flowers) shows *Ready to move after (days)*
  with the usual number for the type, and a planting date. The popup shows the
  stage (Seed, Seedling, Ready to move) and the days left. A plant that is ready
  gets a badge on the zone card and on its overview row, and one phone message.
  A plant you move becomes Established. ZoneFlow never moves anything by itself.

New service: `set_plant_ready` (`plant_id`, `ready_days`, `planted`). `add_plant`
also takes `ready_days` and `planted`. See [docs/AREAS-AND-PLANTS.md](AREAS-AND-PLANTS.md).

## Update your dashboard

No dashboard changes needed. The zone card and the overview card show the new
parts by themselves; do a hard refresh of the browser (Ctrl+F5) to load the new
cards.

## After updating

Nothing: just restart Home Assistant. Plants that are not followed behave as
before.
