# ZoneFlow 1.7.0 — Areas, plants and easier setup

**What's changed**

- **Areas.** Group your zones into the parts of your garden (*Backyard*, *Front
  yard*). An area holds the sensors its zones share: the rain gauge (with the
  size of a tip), the outdoor temperature, the weather entity and the phone.
  A zone uses its area's sensors unless it has one of its own, and its card
  says so. Each area has its own **Pause** and **Snooze Today** (a zone's own
  Pause stays separate) and adds up the litres its zones used. Make one with
  **Add entry → Add an area**, or from a zone's **Configure → Where is this?**
  A **Where is this?** dropdown on every zone card moves a zone between areas
  and greenhouses; its *New area…* and *New greenhouse…* choices create them.
  The overview card and the generated dashboard group the zones by area, and a
  greenhouse with its crops is a group of its own.
- **Greenhouse Pause and Snooze Today.** Pausing a greenhouse (or skipping
  today) now covers its crops' watering. The climate control has its own switch
  and is not affected. The greenhouse's heading on the overview card and its tab
  on the generated dashboard have both buttons.
- **Plants with a history.** Every zone with a valve now has a *main plant*
  (made for you on update; nothing is lost) that keeps its live settings on the
  zone's own sliders as always. The zone card's **Plants** button lists the
  plants and each one's **History** (settings changed, fertilized, notes, moves),
  adds a plant, moves a plant to another zone, makes a record the main plant,
  removes a plant and writes notes. Moving a plant into a zone that already has
  a main plant asks what becomes of that one: it stays as a record, is archived,
  swaps places, or the moved plant joins as a record only. The bed keeps the way
  it is watered unless you tick *Also use this plant's watering settings*. Plants
  can be renamed. Other plants in a zone are records only: they never change the
  watering. A zone with more than one plant lists them on its card, and the card
  shows how the zone is watered.
- **Copy settings and presets.** Give a zone another zone's watering settings,
  or save a zone's settings under a name and use them on any zone later. Adding
  a new zone has a **Start from** choice. The valve, sensors, flow rate, rain
  gauge tip size, planting date and notes are never copied.
- **Check my setup**, a plain checklist on the zone card, and a guided
  **Calibrate** button: run the valve for 15 minutes, enter the litres and the
  area, and the flow rate and Zone Flow are set.
- **Water now for N minutes**, a stepper on the card: safe (not while paused,
  never longer than the zone's runtime cap) and counted in the water record.
- **Why?** under the status shows the numbers behind the next watering. A
  **Simple view** (status, Water now, Snooze, Pause; the rest behind *Advanced
  settings*) is on for newly added cards. A **?** beside settings links to the
  guides. The overview card starts with a **My Garden** summary and can create a
  ready-made ZoneFlow dashboard with one click.
- **Easier setup.** The setup screens list the rain gauges, thermometers,
  weather entities, phones and valves found in Home Assistant with their names
  and values and fill in a clear single match; a Step 2 of 4 counter; plainer
  questions. Three Repairs (a phone that no longer exists, a sensor offline for
  days, a flow rate never set) have **Fix** buttons.
- **Buttons on phone messages** (Home Assistant companion app): a watering
  cancelled for rain, an overdue zone and a fertilizing reminder carry *Water
  10 min*, *Snooze today* and *Fertilized*.
- **A deep soak that comes due soon after a routine watering now waits for the
  next routine slot and replaces it**, instead of watering again the next day (at
  most one routine interval late; *Run Deep Soak Now* still runs at once).

New services: `create_area`, `add_plant`, `move_plant`, `set_main_plant`,
`remove_plant`, `rename_plant`, `add_plant_note`, `save_preset`, `copy_settings`,
`delete_preset`, `water_now`, `calibrate_flow`. See
[docs/AREAS-AND-PLANTS.md](AREAS-AND-PLANTS.md).

## Update your dashboard

The zone card and the overview card show everything new by themselves, and the
ready-made dashboard (`strategy: custom:zoneflow`) gets a tab per area. After
updating, do a hard refresh of the browser (Ctrl+F5) to load the new cards.

A hand-made dashboard can add these entities (new in 1.7.0), for example in an
Entities card (Edit dashboard → the card → Show code editor):

```yaml
type: entities
title: Where is this?
entities:
  - entity: select.<zone>_where_is_this
```

and, for each area you make (the area is a device of its own):

```yaml
type: entities
title: Backyard
entities:
  - entity: switch.<area>_pause
  - entity: button.<area>_snooze_today
  - entity: sensor.<area>_water_used_past_30_days
  - entity: sensor.<area>_water_used_this_year
```

Replace `<zone>` and `<area>` with the entity prefix. Plants, History, Check my
setup, Calibrate and Why? are buttons on the ZoneFlow card, not entities. Each
zone's Status sensor has new attributes (`plants`, `garden_area`, `zone_type`,
`area_note`) for templates. Shortcut: paste `AI_SETUP.md` into an AI assistant
and ask it to update the dashboard for this version.

## After updating

Restart Home Assistant. Every zone with a valve gets its main plant at the
first start; nothing needs doing. If you want areas, make them and put your
zones in them (**Configure → Where is this?**). The Home Assistant companion
app needs its notify service for the buttons on phone messages.
