# ZoneFlow 1.6.2 — Stuck-probe protection and hardening

A small update after 1.6.1, from four weeks of simulated runs and a round of
failure-case tests.

**What's changed**

- **A stuck "dry" soil probe can no longer flood a zone.** A probe that reads
  dry all the time (pulled out of the soil, failed) and keeps reporting made
  the zone water a **full dose every morning** — about four times the weekly
  target in a simulation. A run a dry probe brings forward now gives only the
  share of the dose the days since the last watering call for (at least a
  quarter), so even a stuck probe stays near the weekly target. A real dry
  spell still waters early, just in smaller doses; a probe stuck on "wet"
  that keeps reporting is still believed, and you get the wet-hold alert.
- **Deleting a greenhouse** now switches off its fans, misters and heater and
  closes its vents, since nothing controls them any more. A reload or a
  restart still leaves them alone.
- **Weather Underground:** an answer that isn't a usable report (a list, a
  string, NaN, a missing field) is treated as "no data" instead of causing an
  error, including in the station-picking form; NaN and negative totals are
  never counted as rain.
- A hand-entered rain amount that isn't a finite number is refused; a zone
  that names itself as its greenhouse is just a zone.
- The "nothing is on hold" message of **Resume Automatic** is translated into
  every language.

## Update your dashboard

No dashboard changes needed. After updating, do a hard refresh of the browser
(Ctrl+F5) to load the new card.

## After updating

Restart Home Assistant. Nothing else to do. If you use a soil-moisture probe,
you may notice that a zone brought forward by a dry reading now waters in
smaller doses than before.
