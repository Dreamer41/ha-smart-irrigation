# ZoneFlow 1.7.6 — Look up your plant, crop factors, Rain Ignored Below

**What's new**

- **Look up your plant when you set up a zone.** The first setup screen (and the
  screen for a crop in a greenhouse) has an optional "Look up your plant"
  field: a list that filters as you type, each plant with its usual weekly
  amount of water in your units and its crop factor. Pick one and those become
  the zone's starting values right there. Text that is not in the list is
  searched for on a second screen. What's planted stays the fallback for a
  plant that is not in the list; a pick also chooses the plant type that fits
  (deep soak, growth ramp) when What's planted is left on "Something else".
  Left empty, nothing changes and no screen is added.
- **Watering tips on the zone card** (administrators): a new button that
  looks your plant up and shows how much water it usually wants each week and
  its crop factor. **Apply** sets the normal week to the middle of the range,
  moves the hot and cool weeks in proportion and sets the Crop Factor. Nothing
  changes until you press it.
- **Where the numbers come from.** 1,889 plants with their weekly amounts from
  the [Plant Variety Database](https://github.com/bripatch/plant-variety-database)
  (Wind River Greens, CC BY 4.0); crop factors from FAO paper 56 (Allen et al.
  1998); mango and guava from UF/IFAS, papaya and basil from published studies.
  A plant with nothing of its own gets an **estimated** crop factor from its
  water-use class (low, moderate, high, as landscape plant factors do), and a
  plant with a crop factor but no weekly amount gets one **worked out** from it
  (1 inch a week at a crop factor of 1.0). Estimates are marked "about" or
  "(estimate)" and are rougher than measured values: check the result with
  **Why?**. Succulents, houseplants and microgreens have no weekly amount.
  Credit is in the README, in `data/ATTRIBUTION.md` and on the popup.
- **Rain Ignored Below (per day) is a setting.** A day's rain up to 3 mm has
  always counted as nothing toward the watering. That 3 mm is now a slider in
  the Rain group, 0 to 5 mm, starting at 3 mm so **nothing changes until you
  move it**. Lower it for shallow roots or sandy soil so light rain counts too.
  Copied with Copy settings and presets.
- **Translations:** the new texts are translated into all the languages
  ZoneFlow has.

## Update your dashboard

New entity on every zone:

- `number.<zone>_rain_ignored_below_per_day` (Rain Ignored Below, per day), in
  the **Rain** group of the zone card's **Settings** popup.

The ZoneFlow card shows it there by itself after a hard refresh of the browser
(Ctrl+F5), and the **Watering tips** button appears on the zone card for
administrators. Nothing to add for dashboards built with the ZoneFlow card or
the ZoneFlow dashboard.

A dashboard that lists entities by hand can add it, for example (Edit
dashboard → the card → Show code editor):

```yaml
type: entities
entities:
  - entity: number.<zone>_rain_ignored_below_per_day
```

Replace `<zone>` with the zone's entity prefix (for example `tomato_bed`). The
shortcut: paste AI_SETUP.md into an AI assistant and ask it to update the
dashboard for this version.

## After updating

Nothing: just restart Home Assistant. Existing zones get the new slider at 3 mm,
so the rain credit comes out exactly as before until you change it.
