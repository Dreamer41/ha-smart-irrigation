# ZoneFlow 1.7.7 — A plainer card, and a rain credit that rolls

**What's new**

- **One plain sentence on the garden overview.** Above the chips it now says
  in words how the garden is: "Your garden is doing well.", "Watering now:
  Chilis", "Chilis: needs a look" (amber), "Nothing needs water right now. Rain
  and soil have it covered." or "Watering is paused." It only restates what the
  zones' statuses already say; nothing is decided differently.
- **Recent activity on the zone card.** A new button lists what the zone ran,
  skipped and stopped in the last 10 days, with the reason and the time of each.
  It reads Home Assistant's own history of the zone's status, so nothing new is
  stored.
- **Show details.** The technical numbers of the Now section (litres of the last
  cycle, the weekly target, the 3-day peak temperature, water used, the growth
  ramp) sit behind a "Show details" press, so the card starts with the valve,
  the next watering, the soil and the rain. Nothing was removed.
- **The status names both the next routine and the next deep soak**, and the
  status sensor has two new attributes, `next_routine` and `next_deep_soak`
  (empty while the zone is paused). The routine after a deep soak is dated a
  full interval after it, because a finished deep soak counts as the routine
  watering too.
- **Drying out after rain stays visible.** While the drying hold lasts, the
  status keeps saying so, with when watering resumes, beside later decisions
  such as "counted as done by rain", instead of being replaced by them.
- **Why? shows rain that has already counted.** When rain counted as a
  watering, the rain line says how much rain was counted and how much of it that
  watering already used up, so the credit is not read as all the rain of the
  last days.
- **The rain credit rolls.** Before, when rain counted as a watering, the
  credit forgot the days of rain before it and the card could say "full amount
  needed" in the middle of a wet spell. Now the rain that counted takes one
  dose off the credit, the days of rain before it stay in, a dose leaves with
  its day, and a real watering starts the credit over. The credit shown and the
  watering amount come from the same calculation, so after heavy rain the zone
  skips more than it did. A zone that already carries a rain mark from 1.7.6
  keeps the old way until that mark ends.
- **Rain-counted deep soaks count at most once a calendar day.** More rain the
  same day no longer moves the next deep soak again.
- **Translations:** all new texts are translated into every language ZoneFlow
  has. Two Polish card texts that had Russian words in them are fixed.

**Checked before release:** the full test suite (1,326 tests) on the pinned and
on the latest Home Assistant, hassfest, a card check in a real browser, an
upgrade test from 1.7.6 and a load of a copy of a real 1.7.6 zone's saved data
(nothing reset, the old rain mark still counts), two independent bug hunts
(clock stepped back, saved marks in the future, clock change, restart, imperial
units), and a rain simulation on a sandbox Home Assistant.

## Update your dashboard

No dashboard changes needed. There are no new entities; the status sensor only
gains two attributes. The ZoneFlow card shows the new overview sentence, Recent
activity and Show details by itself after a hard refresh of the browser
(Ctrl+F5).

## After updating

Nothing: just restart Home Assistant. Existing zones keep their saved state and
nothing resets.
